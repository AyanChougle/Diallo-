Customer Service & Support

Delivering 24/7 inbound and outbound customer support, omnichannel query resolution, and elevated customer satisfaction.

Overview & Business Impact

Diallo's Customer Service & Support engine combines trained human specialists, automated call routing, and real-time SLA dashboards to resolve customer issues fast. We manage peak-season spikes seamlessly while maintaining strict Quality Assurance and First Contact Resolution (FCR) targets.

24/7 Multilingual Support

Round-the-clock coverage in English, Hindi, and regional languages to ensure seamless customer experience.

Omnichannel Management

Integrated voice calls, email ticketing, live web chat, and WhatsApp support in a unified queue.

SLA & FCR Guarantee

Continuous monitoring guaranteeing over 92% First Contact Resolution and under 30-second response times.

Quality Monitoring & Analytics

Speech analytics, CSAT sentiment scoring, and automated supervisor evaluation for quality excellence.

Business Use Cases & Applications

High-Volume Query Handling

Managing order tracking, refund inquiries, account updates, and technical troubleshooting smoothly during peak operational hours.

Retention & Customer Engagement

Proactive outbound outreach for customer feedback, re-engagement campaigns, and escalation management.

SaaS & Helpdesk Escalation

Structured Level-1 and Level-2 technical helpdesk support with CRM integration (Zendesk, Salesforce, Freshdesk).

Delivery Framework & Execution

We deploy standard operating protocols engineered for precision and fast onboarding:

1. Discovery & SLA Alignment

Mapping existing ticket flows, call scripts, escalation paths, and defining core KPI benchmarks.

2. Agent Training & Onboarding

Rigorous product knowledge training, voice modulation, and simulator scenario evaluations.

3. Go-Live & Parallel Testing

Phased rollout with continuous supervisor auditing to guarantee zero drop-off during transition.

4. Optimization & Reporting

Weekly executive dashboards, actionable root-cause analysis, and continuous workflow optimization.
